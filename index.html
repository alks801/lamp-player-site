LAMP PLAYER

A retro player for your own music and internet radio. Four skins, cassettes you can swap, a player that turns as you tilt the phone, and keys that click.

Download APK · Android · 0.0.2 Google Play: coming soon

30 MB, for phones with a 64-bit processor (almost every phone since 2017). Android will ask you to allow installing from your browser: that is normal for an app from outside the store.

FOUR SKINS

  • Classic: a desktop-player window with an LCD clock and a spectrum analyzer
  • Cassette deck: the reels turn and the tape winds from one reel to the other
  • 3D player: a portable cassette player with real depth; its door swings open and it turns as you tilt the phone
  • Valve radio: a walnut cabinet, glowing valves, a lit dial and a magic eye

CASSETTES

  • Every music folder and every radio station is a cassette with its own colours
  • Press eject, pick another cassette from the shelf and watch it slide in

YOUR MUSIC AND RADIO

  • MP3, FLAC, M4A/AAC, Ogg, Opus, WAV and more, from the folders you choose
  • Gapless playback, shuffle, repeat, lock screen and headset controls
  • 31 built-in stations in five languages, or any stream address you add
  • 10-band equalizer with presets, and a Stop key that winds down like a tape deck

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your music never leaves your phone. Read the privacy policy.
