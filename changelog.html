VERSION HISTORY

What changed in each version. The latest one is on the home page; it installs over the previous one and keeps your settings and music folders.

0.0.4 November 28, 2026

  • A new skin, Car: the classic look with big keys and big text, for a phone in a car holder. The title of the playing track scrolls when it does not fit
  • Playlist sorting: by name or by file date, either way
  • Keyboard control: space plays and pauses, the arrows step through tracks and set the volume
  • Adding a station puts the cursor straight into the address field

0.0.3 November 28, 2026

  • Shuffle and the order of tracks are fixed
  • The player opens at once, without waiting for the music search
  • Less battery use in the background: with the app minimized or the screen off, the player no longer draws a spectrum nobody can see
  • Adding a folder while music plays no longer sends it back to the start
  • If access to music is refused, the player shows where to turn it on
  • About now names the author and links to the project site

0.0.2 November 28, 2026

  • Cassettes: every music folder and every radio station is now a cassette with its own colours. Swap it with the ⏏ key
  • A new skin, 3D player: a portable cassette player with depth and a door that swings open
  • The Cassette skin's deck has a door too, and its cassettes can be swapped as well
  • The cassette skins respond to tilting the phone; the effect can be turned off in the settings

0.0.1 November 27, 2026

The first version.

  • Your own music from the folders you choose: MP3, FLAC, M4A/AAC, Ogg, Opus, WAV and more
  • 31 built-in radio stations in five languages, and your own by stream address
  • A 10-band equalizer with presets, and a spectrum analyzer
  • Three skins: classic, cassette deck and valve radio
  • Upright and sideways layouts, five interface languages
  • Background playback, lock screen and headset controls
